7.1 Purchase and Activation

By purchasing the Individual Performance Development Programme ($500 one-time fee), the client purchases eight (8) consultations of 45 minutes each, delivered within four (4) consecutive calendar months beginning on the scheduled first consultation date, provided that appointment proceeds or is counted as used under the attendance rules.

The first appointment must be selected after checkout and scheduled to take place within 30 calendar days of purchase. The programme is activated on that qualifying first consultation date. If the practitioner cancels the first appointment, activation moves to the replacement first consultation.

Service Scope Limit: The programme does not include unlimited communication, emergency services, medical treatment, psychiatric medication, or clinical services not expressly listed in the formal offer.

7.2 Seven-Day Cancellation Request

A client may request cancellation and a full refund within seven (7) calendar days after payment, provided no consultation has taken place. Requests must be submitted through the client portal or via direct written notice to mailjoshuaoparachukwu@gmail.com before the seven-day period expires.

Approved refunds will be processed and returned through the original payment method (e.g. Selar / card processor), subject to applicable banking regulations and provider clearing timeframes.

7.3 After the Cancellation Period

After the seven-day cancellation period has elapsed, or immediately once the first consultation has been conducted (whichever comes first), unused consultations are strictly non-refundable. This includes circumstances where a client elects not to schedule or attend remaining available consultations.

A client may pause their participation or cease booking sessions at any time; however, voluntary withdrawal does not create any entitlement to a partial or pro-rata refund.

7.4 Booking, Missed Sessions, 24-Hour Notice and Expiry

Clients must schedule and complete all eight consultations within the four-month programme window (or within an eligible paid 30-day rollover extension). The recommended scheduling rhythm is approximately two sessions per calendar month; however, this is a flexible guide and not an absolute monthly cap. Total eight session credits and the final programme expiry date are strict boundaries.

24-Hour Rescheduling Rule: Consultations may be rescheduled without penalty with at least 24 hours' advance notice prior to the session start time via the client portal. A consultation cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice or missed without notice (no-show) is treated as completed and consumes one (1) session credit, except where an exceptional documented emergency is approved by the practice.

Practitioner-initiated cancellations never consume a client credit; a replacement appointment will be arranged promptly within the active term.

7.5 One-Time Programme Rollover ($100 Fee)

Clients who have completed at least four (4) of their eight consultations prior to programme expiry may opt to purchase one 30-day extension for a single, one-time $100 rollover fee.

Key Rollover Rules:

  • The rollover fee must be paid before the original four-month programme expiry date arrives.
  • The rollover carries forward up to a maximum of four (4) unused consultation credits.
  • The 30-day extension begins immediately after the original expiry date.
  • Only one rollover is permitted per programme engagement.
  • Any credits remaining unused at the conclusion of the 30-day extension lapse permanently. No subsequent rollover, extension, or refund may apply.

7.6 Practitioner and Specialist Allocation

Upon professional assessment, and where an identified performance requirement falls outside the immediate scope or warrants specialized expertise, the practice may recommend that up to two (2) of the eight included consultations be delivered by an appropriately qualified affiliated specialist.

Firm-Funded Specialist Guarantee: The client will never be charged an additional fee for included specialist sessions. The practice contracts and pays the specialist directly. The client is fully informed of the specialist’s credentials and must provide affirmative agreement before any session transfer takes place. External medical diagnostics, pathology tests, prescription drugs, or ongoing specialized treatments beyond the retainer are excluded.

7.7 Safety, Confidentiality and Service Boundaries

This is an applied performance psychology and strategic development service. It is strictly not an emergency medical service, crisis hotline, or psychiatric intervention. Where urgent psychological or physical risks are identified, the practice will refer the individual immediately to emergency services or clinical healthcare providers.

All client conversations and records are held under strict professional confidentiality. Client-facing session notes and shared development summaries are compartmentalized separately from restricted internal practitioner notes.

7.8 Provider Non-Delivery and Disputes

If the practice is unable to deliver a scheduled consultation due to practitioner illness or operational disruption, a reasonable replacement appointment or corresponding programme term extension will be provided. If the practice cannot deliver a material part of the contracted retainer, an appropriate remedy, including a proportionate refund where justified, will be offered.

Clients may submit any administrative inquiries or dispute notifications directly to mailjoshuaoparachukwu@gmail.com. The practice acknowledges inquiries promptly and maintains documented audit logs of all resolutions.
