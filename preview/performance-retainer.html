Questions & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions & Program Rules

What is the cancellation and refund policy? +
Under Section 7.2 of our published terms, you may request cancellation and a full refund within seven (7) calendar days of purchase, provided no consultation has taken place. After seven days or once your first session occurs, the program is non-refundable.
How does rescheduling work? (24-Hour Notice Rule) +
You may reschedule appointments with at least 24 hours’ advance notice directly inside the Client Portal. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice or unattended sessions consume one session credit.
What happens if I have unused sessions at the 4-month mark? +
Unused session credits ordinarily expire at the end of the four-month program. However, clients who have completed at least four (4) sessions may opt to pay a single, one-time $100 rollover fee to extend up to four unused credits for an additional 30 calendar days.
How are affiliated specialist sessions handled? +
If your performance assessment indicates a need for specialized expertise (e.g., specific clinical psychology, biomechanical, or tactical domains), up to two of your eight sessions may be allocated to an affiliated qualified specialist. The practice covers their fees directly, with no extra cost to you, and your affirmative consent is always required prior to booking.
When does the four-month clock actually start? +
Your four-month program clock starts on the date of your qualifying first consultation (which must be scheduled within 30 days of purchase), and expires exactly four calendar months later.
